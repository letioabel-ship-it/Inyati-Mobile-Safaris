Destination 01

Moremi Game Reserve

Located in the heart of the Okavango Delta, Moremi Game Reserve is one of Botswana’s most celebrated wildlife destinations. Known for its diverse landscapes of floodplains, forests, lagoons and waterways, the reserve supports an incredible variety of wildlife.

Visitors can experience unforgettable game drives and boat-based safaris while searching for elephants, lions, leopards, buffalo, wild dogs and numerous bird species. Moremi offers exceptional year-round wildlife viewing and a true African wilderness experience.

Destination 02

Khwai Community Concession

Khwai Community Concession is a remarkable wildlife area managed by the local community and situated adjacent to Moremi Game Reserve in the Okavango Delta region.

Guests can enjoy game drives, night safaris, mokoro trips and cultural experiences across floodplains, woodlands and waterways inhabited by elephants, lions, leopards, wild dogs and abundant birdlife. Visiting Khwai also supports community-based conservation.

Destination 03

Makgadikgadi Pans National Park

Makgadikgadi Pans National Park offers a unique safari experience across one of the largest salt-pan landscapes in the world. This vast wilderness combines dramatic scenery, fascinating geological features and diverse wildlife.

During the dry season, visitors can explore endless open landscapes while discovering elephants, zebras, wildebeest and predators. During the rainy season, the area transforms as thousands of flamingos and other migratory birds arrive.

Destination 04

Savuti Campsite

Savuti is a legendary safari destination within the Chobe National Park ecosystem, famous for its dramatic landscapes, abundant wildlife and unique natural history. The area is renowned for the mysterious Savuti Channel.

Visitors can enjoy exceptional game viewing, with opportunities to encounter elephants, lions, hyenas, buffalo and large concentrations of wildlife around water sources. Savuti Campsite provides an immersive wilderness experience close to nature.

Enquire About Savuti View All Destinations

Destination 05

Chobe National Park

Chobe National Park is one of Botswana’s premier wildlife destinations, famous for its large elephant population and spectacular game-viewing opportunities. Situated along the Chobe River, it combines land and water-based safari experiences.

Visitors can enjoy exciting game drives and peaceful boat cruises while observing elephants, lions, buffalo, hippos, crocodiles and diverse birdlife. Its stunning sunsets and rich ecosystems make Chobe an unforgettable destination.

Destination 06

Victoria Falls

Victoria Falls offers an unforgettable combination of adventure, natural beauty and cultural experiences. Located along the Zambezi River, this spectacular waterfall creates breathtaking views as millions of litres of water cascade over the cliffs.

Visitors can enjoy scenic viewpoints, boat cruises, wildlife encounters, helicopter flights and other adventure excursions. It is the perfect addition to a northern Botswana safari.

Enquire About Victoria Falls View All Destinations

Destination 07

Central Kalahari Game Reserve

The Central Kalahari Game Reserve is one of the largest protected wilderness areas in the world, covering a vast landscape of grasslands, dunes and seasonal river valleys. It provides an authentic off-the-beaten-path safari experience.

The reserve is home to lions, cheetahs, leopards, brown hyenas, giraffes and various antelope species. Visitors can also learn about the rich cultural heritage of the San people who have lived in this region for generations.

Enquire About Central Kalahari View All Destinations

Destination 08

Nxai Pan National Park

Nxai Pan National Park is known for its open grasslands, seasonal waterholes and impressive wildlife encounters. The park is home to zebra, elephants, giraffes, antelope and predators including lions and cheetahs.

One of its most iconic attractions is Baines’ Baobabs, a collection of ancient trees standing dramatically against the vast landscape. Nxai Pan is ideal for wildlife enthusiasts, photographers and adventure seekers.
