Who We Are

Sharing the True Spirit of Botswana

Inyati Mobile Safaris is a Botswana-based tour operator established in Maun in 2020.

We specialise in professionally guided game drives, mobile camping safaris, boat cruises, mokoro excursions, bush walks and other wildlife experiences.

Our Story

Inyati Mobile Safaris was created from a deep enthusiasm for sharing Botswana’s remarkable natural beauty and wildlife. Our purpose is to provide genuine safari experiences while promoting responsible tourism and helping conserve the country’s precious ecosystems.

We help visitors experience the true spirit of Botswana through carefully planned and professionally guided adventures across the Okavango Delta, Moremi Game Reserve, Chobe National Park, Makgadikgadi Pans and Nxai Pan National Park.

As our company continues to grow, we remain committed to outstanding customer service, long-term guest relationships and continuously improving every experience we provide.

Our Strategic Plans

Our Long-Term Direction

Our mission, vision and strategic objectives guide how we provide memorable safari experiences and grow responsibly.

Our Mission

To provide safe, personalised and memorable safari experiences through professional guiding, exceptional customer service and responsible tourism while showcasing Botswana’s unique wildlife, landscapes and cultural heritage.

Our Vision

To become a trusted provider of authentic safari experiences, connecting travellers with the beauty and diversity of Botswana’s wilderness.

Strategic Objectives

To grow sustainably by expanding our safari experiences, strengthening customer relationships, supporting local communities and maintaining high standards of safety, service and environmental responsibility.

Our Guiding Principles

Our Core Values

These principles guide our decisions, relationships and every safari experience we provide.

Conservation

We practise responsible tourism that protects Botswana’s wildlife, natural landscapes and ecosystems for future generations.

Safety

We prioritise the safety and well-being of our guests, guides, staff and the wildlife encountered during every journey.

Excellence

We deliver high-quality safari experiences through professionalism, attention to detail and exceptional customer service.

Integrity

We operate with honesty, transparency and respect toward our guests, employees, communities and business partners.
