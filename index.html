Welcome to Nyati Safaris

Creating Unforgettable Safari Experiences

At Inyati Mobile Safaris, we are passionate about showcasing Botswana’s natural beauty through safe, memorable and professionally guided safari experiences.

Based in Maun, we provide mobile camping safaris, guided game drives, mokoro excursions, boat cruises and bush walks across some of Botswana’s most remarkable wilderness destinations.

Our Mandate

To provide safe, memorable and sustainable safari experiences while protecting Botswana’s natural environment and supporting responsible tourism.
